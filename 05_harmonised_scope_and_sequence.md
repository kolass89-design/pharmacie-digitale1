# OAK INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL
## Deliverable 5: Harmonised Scope and Sequence Plans (Classes CM2 to Première)
**Document Reference:** OIS-MTH-SCQ-2026-V1.0  
**Curricular Scope:** 7 Academic Grade Levels (CM2/MYP 1 to Première/DP 2)  
**Standard Academic Year Model:** Three Instructional Terms (Term 1: September–December; Term 2: January–March; Term 3: April–June)  
**Important Note:** All instructional time allocations (hours/weeks) and unit sequences are institutional pedagogical recommendations designed by our curriculum team to harmonise both syllabi; they do not represent statutory state timetables.

---

### 1. CLASS LEVEL 1: CM2 / MYP 1

#### Term 1: Number Sense, Operations & Geometric Foundations (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 1.1: Whole Numbers, Place Value and the Four Operations**
  - *Proposed Time*: 4 weeks (20 hours).
  - *IB Content & Objectives*: Place value up to millions, standard operations, mental estimation, solving word problems (Criterion A & D).
  - *Benin APC Competencies*: Nombres entiers jusqu'au milliard, techniques opératoires complètes, division euclidienne avec reste, calcul mental rapide.
  - *Shared Conceptual Foundation*: Base-10 positional number system and distributive property of multiplication.
  - *Programme-Specific Extensions*: IB: Criterion B pattern investigations on square numbers. Benin: Word problems with large currency amounts (CFA Francs).
  - *Prerequisites*: Multi-digit addition and multiplication tables.
  - *Bridging Lessons*: Aligning decimal point notation ($12.5$) and decimal comma ($12,5$).
  - *Bilingual Vocabulary*: Place value / *Valeur de position*; Quotient / *Quotient*; Remainder / *Reste*; Dividend / *Dividende*; Divisor / *Diviseur*.
  - *Common Misconceptions*: Forgetting the placeholder zero in long multiplication; ignoring the remainder in division.
  - *Formative Assessments*: Weekly 10-minute mental arithmetic sprint; error-analysis exit tickets.
  - *Assessment Connections*: IB Criterion A & D; Benin $C_m$ & $C_o$.
  - *Proposed Workbook Activities*: "The Cotonou Wholesaler Ledger": balancing commercial supply receipts.

- **Unit 1.2: Fractions and Rational Concepts**
  - *Proposed Time*: 4 weeks (20 hours).
  - *IB Content & Objectives*: Fractions as division, equivalent fractions, comparing fractions, adding/subtracting like denominators.
  - *Benin APC Competencies*: Écriture fractionnaire, simplification, fractions égales, somme et différence de fractions de dénominateurs multiples.
  - *Shared Conceptual Foundation*: Equivalence of parts of a partitioned unit.
  - *Programme-Specific Extensions*: IB: Visual fraction bar models. Benin: Fraction of a whole quantity (e.g., $3/4$ of $240\text{ kg}$).
  - *Prerequisites*: Factors, multiples, division facts.
  - *Bilingual Vocabulary*: Numerator / *Numérateur*; Denominator / *Dénominateur*; Equivalent fraction / *Fraction équivalente*; Irreducible / *Irréductible*.
  - *Common Misconceptions*: Adding denominators together ($\frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{b} = \frac{a+c}{2b}$).
  - *Assessment Connections*: IB Criterion A; Benin $C_o$.
  - *Proposed Workbook Activities*: "Sharing the Harvest": fractional distribution of corn crops in Parakou.

- **Unit 1.3: Planar Geometry: Lines, Angles, and Polygons**
  - *Proposed Time*: 4 weeks (20 hours).
  - *IB Content & Objectives*: Polygons, angle types, protractor measurement, line symmetry.
  - *Benin APC Competencies*: Droites parallèles et perpendiculaires, angles (droit, aigu, obtus, plat), triangles, quadrilatères particuliers, médiatrice.
  - *Shared Conceptual Foundation*: Geometric invariance under translation and reflection.
  - *Programme-Specific Extensions*: IB: Criterion C geometric shape profiles. Benin: Compass-and-straightedge construction protocols.
  - *Prerequisites*: Use of a ruler and pencil.
  - *Bilingual Vocabulary*: Perpendicular / *Perpendiculaire*; Parallel / *Parallèle*; Perpendicular bisector / *Médiatrice*; Acute / *Aigu*; Obtuse / *Obtus*.
  - *Common Misconceptions*: Confusing the inner and outer scale of a circular protractor.
  - *Assessment Connections*: IB Criterion C; Benin $C_m$.
  - *Proposed Workbook Activities*: "Architectural Plans of the Royal Palaces of Abomey": drawing verified orthogonal layouts.

#### Term 2: Decimals, Metric Mensuration & Proportionality (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 1.4: Decimals, Metric System and Conversions**
  - *Proposed Time*: 4 weeks (20 hours).
  - *Shared Foundation*: Decimal place value and metric conversion prefixes (kilo-, hecto-, deca-, deci-, centi-, milli-).
  - *Extensions*: Benin: Agrarian units (ha, a, ca) and volume-capacity-mass equivalence ($1\text{ dm}^3 = 1\text{ L} = 1\text{ kg}$).
  - *Workbook Activity*: "Water Reservoir Calibration in Dassa-Zoumè".

- **Unit 1.5: Perimeter, Area, and 3D Solids**
  - *Proposed Time*: 4 weeks (20 hours).
  - *Shared Foundation*: Perimeter of polygons, area formulas (square, rectangle, triangle, circle), volume of rectangular prisms ($V = L \times w \times h$).
  - *Extensions*: IB: Packing unit cubes investigation (Criterion B). Benin: Trapezius and rhombus area formulas for CEP.
  - *Workbook Activity*: "Fencing and Paving a Market Stall in Dantokpa".

- **Unit 1.6: Proportionality, Percentages and Commercial Maths**
  - *Proposed Time*: 3 weeks (15 hours).
  - *Shared Foundation*: Multiplicative scaling, percentages ($10\%, 25\%, 50\%$).
  - *Extensions*: Benin: Commercial arithmetic formulas (*Prix d'achat, Frais, Prix de revient, Prix de vente, Bénéfice, Perte*).
  - *Workbook Activity*: "The Songhaï Organic Farm Budget".

#### Term 3: Coordinate Grids, Data, Probability & Exam Prep (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 1.7: First Quadrant Coordinates and Statistics** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 1.8: Probability Foundations & Patterns** (2 weeks / 10 h) [IB Focus].
- **Unit 1.9: Integrated CEP & MYP 1 Capstone Review** (4 weeks / 20 h).

### 2. CLASS LEVEL 2: SIXIÈME / MYP 2

#### Term 1: Number Systems, Prime Factors & Synthetic Geometry (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 2.1: Divisibility, Prime Factorisation, HCF & LCM** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Vocabulary*: Prime factor / *Facteur premier*; Divisibility / *Divisibilité*; LCM / *PPCM*; HCF (GCD) / *PGCD*.
- **Unit 2.2: Directed Numbers ($\mathbb{Z}$) [Bridging Unit for MYP 2]** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Bridging Note*: Explicitly introduces negative integers to satisfy MYP 2 early requirements while maintaining Beninese focus on signed magnitude.
- **Unit 2.3: Synthetic Plane Geometry & Geometric Sets** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Benin Focus*: (SA 1) Notions ensemblistes ($\in, \notin, \cap, \cup$), segments, rays, perpendicular bisectors.
- **Unit 2.4: Axial Symmetry (Line Reflection)** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Reflection across straight lines, conservation of distances, angles, and areas.

#### Term 2: Fractions, Algebraic Intro & 3D Mensuration (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 2.5: Fractions: Multiplication, Division & Operations** (4 weeks / 20 h).
- **Unit 2.6: Algebraic Expressions & One-Step Equations** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 2.7: Cuboids, Cubes, Prisms: Nets, Area & Volume** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Benin Focus*: (SA 3) Cavalier perspective, lateral and total surface area, volume formulas.

#### Term 3: Proportionality, Data Handling & Deductive Proof (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 2.8: Proportionality, Scale & Percentage Applications** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 2.9: Statistics: Tables, Bar Charts & Data Interpretation** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 2.10: Formal Geometric Proof Workshops & Year-End Review** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Deductive Protocol*: Mastery of « On sait que... Or si... Donc... ».

### 3. CLASS LEVEL 3: CINQUIÈME / MYP 3

#### Term 1: Directed Numbers, Powers of 10 & Parallel Angles (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 3.1: Operations in Relative Numbers ($\mathbb{Z}$ and Relatives)** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Number line ordering, sign rules, parentheses removal, distance between two points.
- **Unit 3.2: Powers of 10, Index Laws & Scientific Notation** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: $10^n, 10^{-n}$, index addition/subtraction, scientific notation $a \times 10^k$, order of magnitude.
- **Unit 3.3: Angles and Parallel Lines** (5 weeks / 25 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Alternate interior, alternate exterior, and corresponding angles; vertically opposite angles; triangle angle sum ($180^\circ$).

#### Term 2: Fractions, Central Symmetry & Parallelograms (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 3.4: Fractions: Unlike Denominators & Four Operations** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 3.5: Central Symmetry (Point Reflection)** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Point symmetry, center of symmetry, invariant properties.
- **Unit 3.6: Parallelograms & Special Quadrilaterals** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Diagonal bisection, opposite side equality, rhombus, rectangle, square proofs.
- **Unit 3.7: Prisms, Cylinders & Solids of Revolution** (2 weeks / 10 h).

#### Term 3: Proportionality, Data, Probability & Sequences (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 3.8: Proportionality, Rates & Linear Journey Graphs** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 3.9: Statistics: Frequencies, Percentages & Circular Charts** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 3.10: Probability Trees & Linear Sequences [IB Module]** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 3.11: Integrated Problem-Solving Review** (2 weeks / 10 h).

### 4. CLASS LEVEL 4: QUATRIÈME / MYP 4

#### Term 1: Pythagoras' Theorem, Rational Numbers & Exponents (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 4.1: Pythagoras' Theorem and its Converse** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, right-triangle identification, 2D and 3D applications.
- **Unit 4.2: Rational Numbers ($\mathbb{Q}$) and Sign Rules** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 4.3: Integer Exponents and Powers** (2 weeks / 10 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Rules $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$, $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$, $(ab)^n = a^n b^n$, $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$.
- **Unit 4.4: Algebraic Expansion, Single/Double Distributivity** (3 weeks / 15 h).

#### Term 2: Triangle Geometry, Vectors, Translations & Equations (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 4.5: Triangle Concurrency & Midpoint Theorem** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Benin Focus*: Centroid $G$ ($2/3$ rule), orthocentre $H$, circumcentre, incentre.
- **Unit 4.6: Introduction to Vectors and Translations** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Direction, sense, norm; Chasles' relation $\vec{AB} + \vec{BC} = \vec{AC}$; column vectors $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$.
- **Unit 4.7: Linear Equations in One Unknown** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Solving $ax + b = cx + d$, fractional equations, word problem translation.
- **Unit 4.8: Pyramids and Right Cones: Surface & Volume** (2 weeks / 10 h).

#### Term 3: Trigonometry, Statistics & Cumulative Data (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 4.9: Right-Angled Trigonometry ($\cos, \sin, \tan$)** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Harmonised Bridge*: Teach $\sin$ and $\tan$ alongside Benin's prescribed $\cos$.
- **Unit 4.10: Cumulative Statistics, Boxplots & Linear Functions** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 4.11: Integrated Application & Criterion B/D Investigation** (2 weeks / 10 h).

### 5. CLASS LEVEL 5: TROISIÈME / MYP 5

#### Term 1: Thales' Theorem, Real Numbers & Algebraic Identities (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 5.1: Thales' Intercept Theorem & Converse** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Intercept ratios $\frac{AM}{AB} = \frac{AN}{AC} = \frac{MN}{BC}$, similarity, scale factor $k$.
- **Unit 5.2: The Real Number System ($\mathbb{R}$) & Radical Simplification** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Surds, exact calculations, conjugate expressions in denominators.
- **Unit 5.3: Algebraic Identities & Complete Factorisation** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: $(a\pm b)^2, a^2-b^2$, null-factor equations $(ax+b)(cx+d)=0$, rational fractions.

#### Term 2: Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors & Linear Systems (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 5.4: Trigonometric Relations & Special Angles** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Fundamental identities $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$, $\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, values for $30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ$.
- **Unit 5.5: Coordinate Geometry & Vectors in Orthonormal Systems** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Vector coordinates, midpoint, distance formula, collinearity ($xy'-x'y=0$), orthogonality ($xx'+yy'=0$).
- **Unit 5.6: Affine Functions & Straight Line Equations** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: $y = mx + p$, slope calculation, parallel and perpendicular slopes.
- **Unit 5.7: Linear Systems ($2 \times 2$) & Problem Formulation** (2 weeks / 10 h).

#### Term 3: Circle Geometry, 3D Frustums, Statistics & Dual Exam Clinics (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 5.8: Inscribed Angles, Circle Geometry & 3D Frustums** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 5.9: Grouped Continuous Statistics & Histograms** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 5.10: Sine/Cosine Rules & Quadratic Formula [IB Extension]** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 5.11: Intensive BEPC & MYP 5 Dual Examination Practicum** (3 weeks / 15 h).

### 6. CLASS LEVEL 6: SECONDE / DP 1

#### Term 1: Real Numbers, Quadratics & Functional Analysis (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 6.1: Subsets of $\mathbb{R}$, Intervals, and Absolute Value** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 6.2: Quadratic Trinomials, Discriminant $\Delta$ & Inequations** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$, roots, factorisation, vertex form, sign tables.
- **Unit 6.3: General Concept of Function & Graph Transformations** (5 weeks / 25 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Domain $D_f$, parity, monotonicity, extrema, reference functions, transformations $y = af(b(x-c))+d$.

#### Term 2: 2D Vector Spaces, Dot Product & Radian Trigonometry (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 6.4: Vector Spaces, Bases & Cartesian Equations of Lines** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 6.5: Scalar Product (Dot Product) & Metric Relations** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Dot product definition, coordinates ($xx'+yy'$), Al-Kashi theorem, median theorem, circle equations.
- **Unit 6.6: Radian Measure, Unit Circle & Trigonometric Identities** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Radians, circular functions, addition and double-angle formulas, exact values.

#### Term 3: Sequences, Logarithms, 3D Space & Statistics (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 6.7: Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences and Series [DP 1 Core]** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 6.8: 3D Incidence Geometry & Orthogonality [Benin 2nde C/D Core]** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 6.9: Introductory Logarithms & Exponential Equations [DP 1 Core]** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 6.10: Univariate & Bivariate Statistics + GDC Practicum** (2 weeks / 10 h).

### 7. CLASS LEVEL 7: PREMIÈRE / DP 2

#### Term 1: Differential Calculus, Curve Sketching & Sequences (12 Weeks)
- **Unit 7.1: Differential Calculus: Derivative at a Point & Tangents** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Difference quotient, derivative function, power/product/quotient rules, chain rule.
- **Unit 7.2: Limits, Asymptotes & Complete Functional Curve Sketching** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Indeterminate forms, vertical/horizontal/oblique asymptotes, variation tables, graphing.
- **Unit 7.3: Numerical Sequences: Monotonicity, Bounds & Convergence** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Arithmetic and geometric sequences, sum formulas, financial modelling.

#### Term 2: Barycentres, Combinatorics, Probability & Trigonometry (11 Weeks)
- **Unit 7.4: Barycentres of Weighted Points & Level Curves [Benin 1ère C/D Priority]** (4 weeks / 20 h).
  - *Benin Core*: Existence condition $\sum \alpha_i \neq 0$, reduction $\sum \alpha_i \vec{MA_i} = (\sum \alpha_i)\vec{MG}$, partial barycentres, level curves.
- **Unit 7.5: Combinatorics, Permutations & Combinations** (3 weeks / 15 h).
  - *Shared Core*: $p$-lists, arrangements $A_n^p$, combinations $\binom{n}{p}$, Pascal's triangle, Binomial theorem.
- **Unit 7.6: Probability Distributions & Discrete Random Variables** (2 weeks / 10 h).
  - *Shared Core*: Conditional probability, independence, probability distributions, $E(X)$, $\text{Var}(X)$, Binomial distribution.
- **Unit 7.7: Advanced Trigonometric Equations & Simpson Formulas** (2 weeks / 10 h).

#### Term 3: Integration, DP Internal Assessment & Baccalauréat Clinics (9 Weeks)
- **Unit 7.8: Integral Calculus: Anti-derivatives & Definite Integrals [DP 2 Core]** (3 weeks / 15 h).
- **Unit 7.9: Linear Programming via Half-Planes [Benin 1ère Core]** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 7.10: DP Internal Assessment (Exploration) Finalisation** (2 weeks / 10 h).
- **Unit 7.11: Baccalauréat Première & DP Mock Examination Practicum** (2 weeks / 10 h).
