### WORKBOOK STRUCTURE (SECTIONS A THROUGH H)

Every one of the seven activity workbooks strictly adheres to the mandated 8-section pedagogical blueprint:
- **Section A — Student Introduction**: Explains the dual-curriculum philosophy and instructions in age-appropriate, encouraging bilingual language.
- **Section B — Bilingual Mathematical Language**: Complete technical English–French glossary featuring exact definitions, symbols, common false friends, and contextual nuances.
- **Section C — Shared Conceptual Foundations**: Core mathematical theory, underlying proofs, and side-by-side comparative worked examples.
- **Section D — Contextualised Bridging Activities**: Structured 6-step bridging tasks (Situation, IB perspective, Beninese APC perspective, comparison of methods, student reflection, and application in a new context) set in authentic Beninese and international environments.
- **Section E — Progressive Practice (Five Levels)**:
  - *Level 1*: Foundational understanding (definitions, routine single-step operations);
  - *Level 2*: Standard application (core multi-step problems);
  - *Level 3*: Multi-step problem solving;
  - *Level 4*: Reasoning, investigation, and geometric/algebraic proofs;
  - *Level 5*: Programme-specific challenge & high-stakes national examination tasks.
- **Section F — Integrated Application Tasks**: Authentic interdisciplinary investigations evaluated under MYP Criteria B & D or DP Modelling frameworks.
- **Section G — Programme-Specific Reinforcement**: Uncompromised dual-track preparation:
  - Part G-1: IB Criterion Tasks & DP Examination Papers;
  - Part G-2: Beninese National Exam Tasks (CEP, BEPC, and Baccalauréat Séries C/D).
- **Section H — Assessment, Reflection & Complete Worked Solutions**: Diagnostic pre-flight checklists, error analysis, self-reflection prompts, and complete, step-by-step worked solutions with mark breakdowns.
