### 3. OUTSTANDING DECISIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR OAK LEADERSHIP

To operationalise this harmonisation framework effectively for the upcoming academic year, the school leadership should review and confirm three institutional decisions:

#### Decision 1: Formal Selection of the IB Diploma Mathematics Pathway
- **Current Provision**: This framework incorporates the 60-hour shared Common Core, with deep analytical calculus and proof (supporting **Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches - AA**) alongside statistical and modelling tasks (supporting **Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation - AI**).
- **Recommendation**: For students entering scientific streams (aiming for Beninese Série C/D and international engineering/pre-med faculties), Oak should register **Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (AA)** at Standard and Higher Level. If a humanities/business cohort emerges, **Mathematics: AI SL** can be scheduled concurrently.

#### Decision 2: Timetable Allocation for Dual-Track Assessment Clinics
- **Current Provision**: Our harmonised scope-and-sequence allocates 65% of classroom time to the shared conceptual core, 15–20% to IB inquiry and criteria tasks, and 15–20% to Beninese national exam workshops.
- **Recommendation**: In the exam years (**CM2** for the CEP, **Troisième** for the BEPC, and **Première** for Baccalauréat benchmarks), schedule a dedicated 2-hour weekly "Dual-Exam Practicum" where students practice timed non-calculator national exam problems under authentic exam conditions.

#### Decision 3: Standardisation of Graphic Display Calculators (GDC)
- **Current Provision**: Beninese examinations prohibit programmable and graphic calculators (allowing only basic scientific calculators), whereas IB DP examinations mandate Graphic Display Calculators (Paper 2 & 3).
- **Recommendation**: Mandate a single school-wide approved GDC model for all students entering Seconde / DP 1 (e.g., the **Texas Instruments TI-84 Plus CE** or **TI-Nspire CX II**). Maintain strict classroom protocols: students must solve theoretical problems by hand first (satisfying Beninese requirements) before verifying and exploring via GDC (satisfying IB requirements).
