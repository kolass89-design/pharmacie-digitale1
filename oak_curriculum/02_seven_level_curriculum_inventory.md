# OAK INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL
## Deliverable 2: Seven-Level Mathematics Curriculum Content Inventory
**Document Reference:** OIS-MTH-INV-2026-V1.0  
**Curricular Scope:** IB MYP 1–5 & DP 1–2 / Beninese National APC (CM2 to Première)  
**Taxonomic Key:**
- **[INT] Introduced**: Concept or skill formally encountered for the first time.
- **[DEV] Developed**: Existing concept expanded in depth, scope, and technical complexity.
- **[CON] Consolidated**: Concept reinforced to automaticity and procedural mastery.
- **[EXT] Extended**: Advanced or enrichment material beyond the standard baseline.
- **[NP] Not Prescribed**: Not part of the official statutory requirement at this grade.

---

### LEVEL 1: CM2 / MYP 1 (Ages 10–11)

#### 1.1 IB MYP 1 Inventory (English Instruction)
1. **Number systems & operations**: [CON] Positive integers, place value up to millions, standard algorithms for four operations.
2. **Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios & rates**: [DEV] Fractions as division and parts of a whole; equivalent fractions; adding/subtracting fractions with like/related denominators; decimal place value to thousandths; simple percentages ($10\%, 25\%, 50\%$); introduction to unitary ratio and rates.
3. **Powers, roots & standard form**: [INT] Concept of square numbers ($1, 4, 9, 16, \dots$); powers of 10 in place value notation; numerical rounding and estimation strategies.
4. **Number theory**: [DEV] Multiples, factors, prime numbers up to 100, divisibility rules for 2, 3, 5, 9, 10; lowest common multiple (LCM) and highest common factor (HCF).
5. **Algebraic expressions & identities**: [INT] Arithmetic patterns, input-output function machines, expressing simple rules in words and informal symbols (e.g., $\Delta + 3 = 8$ or $n + 3$).
6. **Equations & inequalities**: [INT] Solving one-step missing-number equations by inverse operations.
7. **Sequences & patterns**: [DEV] Arithmetic sequences of integers, identifying term-to-term rules and spatial pattern growth.
8. **Functions & graphs**: [INT] Plotting coordinate points in the first quadrant ($(x, y)$ where $x, y \ge 0$); reading simple linear relationship graphs.
9. **Coordinate geometry**: [INT] First quadrant Cartesian grid, identifying axes and coordinates.
10. **Plane geometry & constructions**: [DEV] Properties of 2D shapes (triangles, quadrilaterals, regular polygons); angle types (acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex); angle sum of triangle ($180^\circ$); measuring angles with a protractor.
11. **Measurement, scale, area & volume**: [DEV] Metric units of length, mass, and volume/capacity; perimeter of polygons; area of rectangles and composite rectangular shapes; volume of rectangular prisms by unit cube packing.
12. **Trigonometry**: [NP]
13. **Vectors**: [NP]
14. **Transformations**: [INT] Line symmetry (reflection) and simple rotational symmetry in 2D shapes.
15. **Statistics & data representation**: [DEV] Tally charts, frequency tables, pictograms, bar graphs, double bar graphs; calculating mode, median, and mean of small discrete datasets.
16. **Probability**: [INT] Probability scale from impossible ($0$) to certain ($1$); describing likelihood of single events using words and simple fractions.
17. **Mathematical logic & proof**: [INT] Identifying patterns, formulating verbal conjectures, checking whether a rule works.
18. **Calculus**: [NP]
19. **Mathematical modelling**: [DEV] Real-life word problems involving money, time, and distance.
20. **Digital tools**: [INT] Simple spreadsheets and educational geometry software (e.g., GeoGebra basic tools).

7. **Suites & motifs**: [DEV] Suites numériques arithmétiques simples dans les activités de calcul mental.

### LEVEL 2: SIXIÈME / MYP 2 (Ages 11–12)

#### 2.1 IB MYP 2 Inventory (English Instruction)
1. **Number systems & operations**: [DEV] Introduction to negative integers, ordering integers on a number line, addition and subtraction of directed numbers.
2. **Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios & rates**: [DEV] Multiplying and dividing fractions; converting between fractions, decimals, and percentages; dividing a quantity in a given ratio; rates and unit prices.
3. **Powers, roots & standard form**: [DEV] Index notation for positive integer powers ($a^n$); square roots and cube roots of square and cube numbers; order of operations (BIDMAS/PEMDAS).
4. **Number theory**: [CON] Prime factorisation (index form), prime factor trees, finding LCM and HCF by prime factors.
5. **Algebraic expressions & identities**: [INT] Formulating algebraic expressions, identifying terms, coefficients, variables, and constants; simplifying algebraic expressions by collecting like terms; expanding single brackets ($a(b+c) = ab + ac$).
6. **Equations & inequalities**: [DEV] Solving linear equations with one unknown involving two steps (e.g., $ax + b = c$); representing simple inequalities on a number line ($x > 2$).
7. **Sequences & patterns**: [DEV] Linear sequences, finding the $n^{\text{th}}$ term rule ($un = dn + c$).
8. **Functions & graphs**: [INT] Plotting linear graphs from a table of values in all four quadrants; identifying the gradient and intercept intuitively.
9. **Coordinate geometry**: [DEV] Four-quadrant Cartesian plane, coordinates $(x, y)$, plotting and interpreting geometric shapes on grids.
10. **Plane geometry & constructions**: [DEV] Angle relationships (supplementary, complementary, vertically opposite, angles at a point); angle properties of parallel lines cut by a transversal (alternate, corresponding, interior); constructing triangles with ruler, compass, and protractor.
11. **Measurement, scale, area & volume**: [DEV] Circumference and area of circles; area of parallelograms and trapeziums; surface area and volume of right prisms and cylinders.
12. **Trigonometry**: [NP]
13. **Vectors**: [NP]
14. **Transformations**: [DEV] Reflections in horizontal and vertical lines ($x=k, y=k$); translations using column vectors; rotations about a centre point.
15. **Statistics & data representation**: [DEV] Discrete vs continuous data; grouped frequency tables; histograms with equal intervals; calculating the range, mean, median, and mode.
16. **Probability**: [DEV] Theoretical probability of equally likely single outcomes; complementary events ($P(A') = 1 - P(A)$); experimental probability and relative frequency.
17. **Mathematical logic & proof**: [DEV] Verifying geometric properties, justifying steps in angle calculations using formal geometric reasons.
18. **Calculus**: [NP]
19. **Mathematical modelling**: [DEV] Investigating real-life relationships (Criterion D tasks: consumer mathematics, environmental measurements).
20. **Digital tools**: [DEV] Use of dynamic geometry software (GeoGebra) to investigate angle properties; basic spreadsheet formulas.

6. **Équations**: [INT] Équations de la forme $x + a = b$, $a - x = b$, $a \times x = b$ ($a \neq 0$) sur les décimaux.

11. **Grandeurs, mesures, aires & volumes**: [DEV] (SA 3 & 4) Périmètre et aire des figures usuelles (rectangle, carré, triangle, disque); pavé droit et cube: perspective cavalière, patrons, calculs de l'aire latérale, aire totale et volume ($V = a^3$, $V = L \times l \times h$).

15. **Statistiques**: [DEV] (SA 4) Tableaux de données statistiques, effectifs, diagrammes en bâtons, diagrammes circulaires élémentaires.
16. **Probabilités**: [NP]

19. **Modélisation**: [DEV] Situations d'apprentissage (SA) ancrées dans des contextes socio-économiques béninois.

### LEVEL 3: CINQUIÈME / MYP 3 (Ages 12–13)

#### 3.1 IB MYP 3 Inventory (English Instruction)
1. **Number systems & operations**: [CON] Mastery of operations with all rational numbers (positive and negative integers, fractions, and decimals).
2. **Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios & rates**: [CON] Compound percentages, percentage increase and decrease, reverse percentages; compound units (density, speed, unit rates).
3. **Powers, roots & standard form**: [DEV] Laws of indices with integer exponents ($a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$, $a^m / a^n = a^{m-n}$, $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$, $a^0 = 1$); standard form / scientific notation for large and small numbers ($a \times 10^k$ where $1 \le a < 10, k \in \mathbb{Z}$).
4. **Number theory**: [CON] Prime factorisation, index notation, applications to square and cube roots.
5. **Algebraic expressions & identities**: [DEV] Expansion of single and double brackets: $(x+a)(x+b)$; factorisation by taking out common factors; introduction to simple algebraic fractions.
6. **Equations & inequalities**: [DEV] Solving linear equations with unknowns on both sides: $ax + b = cx + d$; solving linear equations involving fractions; solving and graphing linear inequalities on a number line.
7. **Sequences & patterns**: [DEV] Investigating patterns in tables and diagrams (Criterion B); deriving explicit general rules for linear and simple quadratic sequences.
8. **Functions & graphs**: [DEV] Concept of a function as a mapping; graphing linear functions $y = mx + c$; determining gradient ($m$) and $y$-intercept ($c$); parallel lines.
9. **Coordinate geometry**: [DEV] Midpoint of a segment: $(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2})$; distance between two points via the Pythagorean theorem.
10. **Plane geometry & constructions**: [DEV] Angle sums in convex polygons ($(n-2) \times 180^\circ$); exterior angle theorem; properties of isosceles, equilateral triangles and special quadrilaterals; scale drawings and bearings.
11. **Measurement, scale, area & volume**: [DEV] Surface area and volume of right prisms, cylinders, and composite solids; conversion of compound area and volume units ($\text{m}^2 \leftrightarrow \text{cm}^2$, $\text{m}^3 \leftrightarrow \text{cm}^3$).
12. **Trigonometry**: [INT] Introduction to right-angled triangle side ratios (conceptual preparation for sine, cosine, tangent).
13. **Vectors**: [INT] Column vectors, addition and subtraction of vectors graphically and algebraically; scalar multiplication.
14. **Transformations**: [DEV] Enlargements (dilations) with positive integer scale factors; combinations of transformations.
15. **Statistics & data representation**: [DEV] Two-way tables; stem-and-leaf plots; comparing data distributions using mean, median, mode, and range.
16. **Probability**: [DEV] Sample space diagrams; two-stage probability experiments; tree diagrams for independent events.
17. **Mathematical logic & proof**: [DEV] Formulating conjectures, testing counterexamples, constructing formal geometric deductions.
18. **Calculus**: [NP]
19. **Mathematical modelling**: [DEV] Real-life linear models (Criterion D): cost functions, linear journey graphs.
20. **Digital tools**: [DEV] GeoGebra dynamic transformations; spreadsheet data analysis.

4. **Arithmétique & divisibilité**: [CON] Utilisation des critères de divisibilité pour la simplification des fractions.
5. **Expressions algébriques**: [INT] Développement d'une expression littérale simple: $k(a+b) = ka + kb$; factorisation simple: $ka + kb = k(a+b)$.
6. **Équations**: [DEV] Résolution d'équations simples du premier degré à une inconnue du type $x + a = b$ et $ax = b$ dans l'ensemble des décimaux relatifs.
7. **Suites & motifs**: [NP]
8. **Fonctions & graphiques**: [INT] Repérage d'un point dans le plan par deux coordonnées cartésiennes; lecture et tracé de courbes représentatives de phénomènes physiques ou économiques.

10. **Géométrie plane & constructions**: [DEV] (SA 2) Angles alternes-internes, alternes-externes, correspondants formés par deux droites parallèles et une sécante; somme des mesures des angles d'un triangle égale à $180^\circ$; inégalité triangulaire et construction de triangles; droites remarquables du triangle: médiatrices et cercle circonscrit, bissectrices et cercle inscrit; quadrilatères particuliers: parallélogramme (définition, centre de symétrie, côtés opposés, diagonales), rectangle, losange, carré.

12. **Trigonométrie**: [NP]
13. **Vecteurs**: [NP]
14. **Transformations**: [DEV] (SA 4) Symétrie centrale (par rapport à un point): définition, construction, propriétés de conservation (alignement, longueurs, parallélisme, angles, aires); centre de symétrie d'une figure.
15. **Statistiques**: [DEV] (SA 5) Organisation des données: population, individu, caractère statistique (qualitatif, quantitatif); effectifs, fréquences, fréquences en pourcentage; représentations graphiques (diagrammes circulaires, semi-circulaires, diagrammes en bandes).
16. **Probabilités**: [NP]
17. **Logique & raisonnement**: [DEV] Rédaction structurée des raisonnements géométriques déductifs à partir des propriétés des angles et du parallélogramme.
18. **Calcul infinitésimal**: [NP]
19. **Modélisation**: [DEV] Résolution de situations d'apprentissage relatives à l'aménagement du territoire, commerce et artisanat.
20. **Outils numériques**: [NP]

### LEVEL 4: QUATRIÈME / MYP 4 (Ages 13–14)

#### 4.1 IB MYP 4 Inventory (English Instruction)
1. **Number systems & operations**: [CON] Classification of numbers into sets ($\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R}\setminus\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R}$); operations on rational numbers.
2. **Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios & rates**: [CON] Direct and inverse proportion; financial mathematics (compound interest: $A = P(1 + r/100)^t$, depreciation, currency exchange).
3. **Powers, roots & standard form**: [DEV] Fractional exponents ($a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}$, $a^{m/n} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$); operations with numbers in standard form; simplifying numerical surds ($\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a}\sqrt{b}$).
4. **Number theory**: [DEV] Set notation (union $\cup$, intersection $\cap$, complement, subsets); Venn diagrams with two and three sets.
5. **Algebraic expressions & identities**: [DEV] Expanding double brackets $(ax+b)(cx+d)$; special products: $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$, $(a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$, $(a-b)(a+b) = a^2 - b^2$; factorisation of quadratic expressions $x^2 + bx + c$ and difference of two squares; simplifying rational expressions.
6. **Equations & inequalities**: [DEV] Solving linear equations with fractional coefficients; solving simultaneous linear equations in two variables ($2 \times 2$) by substitution and elimination; solving quadratic equations by factorisation ($x^2 + bx + c = 0$); solving linear inequalities and graphing solution sets on a Cartesian plane.
7. **Sequences & patterns**: [DEV] Arithmetic and geometric sequences; finding formulas for general terms; investigating spatial patterns and proving conjectures (Criterion B).
8. **Functions & graphs**: [DEV] Functional notation $f(x)$; domain and range; linear functions $y = mx + c$; quadratic graphs $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ (axis of symmetry, vertex, intercepts).
9. **Coordinate geometry**: [DEV] Equation of a straight line through two points; parallel and perpendicular line conditions ($m_1 = m_2$, $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$).
10. **Plane geometry & constructions**: [DEV] Congruence criteria (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS); similarity criteria (AAA, SAS, SSS); scale factor of area ($k^2$) and volume ($k^3$).
11. **Measurement, scale, area & volume**: [DEV] Pyramids, cones, and spheres: surface areas and volumes ($V_{\text{pyramid}} = \frac{1}{3}Bh$, $V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$, $V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$).
12. **Trigonometry**: [INT] Right-angled triangle trigonometry: definitions of $\sin \theta$, $\cos \theta$, $\tan \theta$; finding missing sides and angles; angles of elevation and depression.
13. **Vectors**: [DEV] Vector magnitude $\|\vec{v}\| = \sqrt{x^2+y^2}$; vector addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication in component form; simple vector proofs.
14. **Transformations**: [DEV] Enlargements with fractional and negative scale factors; combinations of translations, reflections, rotations, and dilations.
15. **Statistics & data representation**: [DEV] Cumulative frequency tables and curves; median, quartiles ($Q_1, Q_3$), interquartile range (IQR); box-and-whisker plots; scatter plots, correlation, and informal line of best fit.
16. **Probability**: [DEV] Probability of mutually exclusive and non-mutually exclusive events ($P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$); conditional probability from two-way tables and Venn diagrams.
17. **Mathematical logic & proof**: [DEV] Direct deductive proof in geometry and algebra; verifying general expressions algebraically.
18. **Calculus**: [NP]
19. **Mathematical modelling**: [DEV] Constructing linear and quadratic models of authentic physical and economic phenomena (Criterion D).
20. **Digital tools**: [DEV] Spreadsheets for statistical calculations; graphing software (Desmos/GeoGebra) to explore graph transformations.

#### 4.2 Beninese APC Quatrième (4ème) Inventory (French Instruction)
1. **Systèmes de nombres & opérations**: [CON] Nombres rationnels ($\mathbb{Q}$): opérations complètes ($+$, $-$, $\times$, $\div$), écriture sous forme de fraction irréductible, comparaison, signe d'un produit et d'un quotient.
2. **Fractions, décimaux & proportionnalité**: [CON] Calculs avec des fractions rationnelles; proportionnalité directe et inverse; coefficient de proportionnalité; applications économiques.

6. **Équations**: [DEV] Équations du premier degré à une inconnue du type $ax + b = c$ et $ax + b = cx + d$ dans $\mathbb{Q}$; mise en équation et résolution de problèmes concrets.
7. **Suites & motifs**: [NP]
8. **Fonctions & graphiques**: [INT] (SA 5) Applications linéaires: définition $x \mapsto ax$, coefficient de proportionnalité, tableau de valeurs, représentation graphique dans un repère (droite passant par l'origine).

14. **Transformations**: [DEV] Translation: définition à partir d'un vecteur; image d'un point, d'un segment, d'une droite, d'un cercle par une translation; conservation des longueurs, angles, parallélisme et aires.
15. **Statistiques**: [DEV] (SA 5) Effectifs, effectifs cumulés croissants; fréquences, fréquences cumulées; moyenne arithmétique simple et moyenne arithmétique pondérée.
16. **Probabilités**: [NP]
17. **Logique & raisonnement**: [DEV] Consolidation de la démonstration formelle: réciproque de théorèmes géométriques (réciproque de Pythagore, caractérisation du triangle rectangle par son cercle circonscrit).
18. **Calcul infinitésimal**: [NP]
19. **Modélisation**: [DEV] Situations d'apprentissage (SA) intégrant des calculs de charpentes (Pythagore), terrassements et métrologie villageoise.
20. **Outils numériques**: [INT] Calculatrice scientifique standard non programmable.

### LEVEL 5: TROISIÈME / MYP 5 (Ages 14–15)

   - Définition d'une suite $(u_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$: formule explicite ($u_n = f(n)$) ou relation de récurrence ($u_{n+1} = g(u_n)$).
   - Sens de variation d'une suite (calcul de $u_{n+1} - u_n$ ou comparaison du rapport $\frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}$ à 1).
   - Suites majorées, minorées, bornées.
   - Suites arithmétiques: définition par récurrence $u_{n+1} = u_n + r$, terme général $u_n = u_p + (n-p)r$, somme des termes consécutifs $S = \frac{\text{nombre de termes}}{2}(\text{premier terme} + \text{dernier terme})$.
   - Suites géométriques: définition $u_{n+1} = q \times u_n$, terme général $u_n = u_p \times q^{n-p}$, somme des termes consécutifs $S = \text{premier terme} \times \frac{1 - q^{\text{nombre de termes}}}{1 - q}$ ($q \neq 1$).
6. **Trigonométrie approfondie**: [DEV]
   - Équations trigonométriques: $\cos x = \cos \alpha \iff x = \pm \alpha + 2k\pi$; $\sin x = \sin \alpha \iff x = \alpha + 2k\pi \text{ ou } x = \pi - \alpha + 2k\pi$; $\tan x = \tan \alpha \iff x = \alpha + k\pi$.
   - Formules de transformation de produits en sommes et de sommes en produits (formules de Simpson): $\cos p + \cos q = 2 \cos\frac{p+q}{2} \cos\frac{p-q}{2}$, $\sin p + \sin q = 2 \sin\frac{p+q}{2} \cos\frac{p-q}{2}$.
   - Équations de la forme $a \cos x + b \sin x = c$ par transformation en $r \cos(x - \varphi)$.
7. **Angles orientés & produit scalaire**: [DEV]
   - Mesure principale d'un angle orienté de vecteurs $(\vec{u}, \vec{v}) \in ]-\pi, \pi]$.
   - Relations fondamentales de Chasles sur les angles orientés.
   - Produit scalaire dans l'espace: orthogonalité de vecteurs dans l'espace.
8. **Dénombrement & Calcul des Probabilités**: [DEV]
   - Dénombrement: principe additif, principe multiplicatif (produit cartésien); $p$-listes (avec répétition: $n^p$); arrangements sans répétition ($A_n^p = \frac{n!}{(n-p)!}$); permutations ($n!$); combinaisons ($C_n^p = \binom{n}{p} = \frac{n!}{p!(n-p)!}$); formule du triangle de Pascal ($C_n^p = C_{n-1}^p + C_{n-1}^{p-1}$); binôme de Newton.
   - Probabilités sur un univers fini $\Omega$: équiprobabilité ($P(A) = \frac{\text{Card}(A)}{\text{Card}(\Omega)}$); probabilité de l'union $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$; probabilité conditionnelle ($P(B|A)$ ou $P_A(B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(A)}$); formule des probabilités totales; événements indépendants.
   - Variables aléatoires discrètes: loi de probabilité de $X$, espérance mathématique $E(X) = \sum x_i p_i$, variance $V(X) = \sum (x_i - E(X))^2 p_i = \sum x_i^2 p_i - [E(X)]^2$, écart-type $\sigma(X) = \sqrt{V(X)}$.

   - Isométries et homothéties du plan (propriétés de conservation).
