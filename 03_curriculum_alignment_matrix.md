# OAK INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL
## Deliverable 3: Curriculum Alignment Matrix (IB Continuum vs. Beninese APC)
**Document Reference:** OIS-MTH-ALN-2026-V1.0  
**Curricular Scope:** All Seven Oak International School Classes (CM2/MYP 1 to Première/DP 2)  
**Standardised Alignment Taxonomy:**
- **[DIR] Direct Alignment**: Substantially the same mathematical concept, comparable depth, and parallel cognitive expectations.
- **[PAR] Partial Alignment**: Common core concept, but differing in depth, pedagogical method, mathematical notation, or problem context.
- **[SEQ] Sequencing Mismatch**: Same concept taught at different chronological grades in the two programmes.
- **[IB-SPEC] IB-Specific Content**: Prescribed in the IB syllabus but absent from the corresponding Beninese national grade.
- **[APC-SPEC] APC-Specific Content**: Mandated by the Beninese national syllabus but absent from the corresponding IB level.
- **[GAP] Prerequisite Gap**: Concept required as assumed prior knowledge in one programme but not yet taught in the other.
- **[UNV] Unverified**: Insufficient official statutory evidence to establish a definitive curricular correspondence.

#### Detailed Comparative Analysis (CM2 / MYP 1)
- **Similarities**: Common core mastery of elementary arithmetic, multi-digit operations, standard metric conversions, and perimeter/area calculations for standard polygons.
- **Depth Differences**: Beninese CM2 requires high calculation speed without calculators for compound operations, fractions, and multi-step commercial arithmetic problems. MYP 1 emphasizes conceptual representation, inquiry into patterns (Criterion B), and verbal justifications.
- **Terminology & Notation**:
  - English: *area*, *perimeter*, *simplifying fractions*, *decimal point* ($3.14$).
  - French: *aire*, *périmètre*, *rendre irréductible une fraction*, *virgule décimale* ($3,14$).
- **Assessment Differences**: CEP tests structured step-by-step solutions (*Ce que je sais, ce que je cherche, solution, opérations, résultat*). MYP 1 evaluates against Criteria A, B, C, D with open-ended tasks and reflections.
- **Harmonisation Strategy**: Co-teach shared concepts (operations, fractions, geometric formulas); use dual-language terminology flashcards; conduct bilingual bridging tasks linking Beninese market calculations (*achats, ventes, bénéfice*) to MYP Criterion D real-life investigations.

#### Detailed Comparative Analysis (Sixième / MYP 2)
- **Similarities**: High convergence in synthetic planar geometry (segments, lines, angles, circles), axial symmetry, and basic 3D solids.
- **Depth Differences**: Beninese 6ème insists heavily on formal geometric deductive writing (*démonstration géométrique*) using axiomatic phrasing. MYP 2 pushes earlier into negative directed numbers and algebraic expressions.
- **Terminology & Notation**:
  - English: *perpendicular bisector*, *line segment* $[AB]$, *ray* $[AB)$, *midpoint*.
  - French: *médiatrice*, *segment* $[AB]$, *demi-droite* $[AB)$, *milieu*.
- **Sequencing Mismatch Strategy**: Deliver a 2-week bridging unit in MYP 2 on directed numbers to ensure students meet IB expectations without disrupting the Beninese positive-number progression.

#### Detailed Comparative Analysis (Quatrième / MYP 4)
- **Similarities**: Complete shared commitment to Pythagoras' theorem, rational number arithmetic, algebraic expansion, and right pyramid/cone mensuration.
- **Divergence**:
  - Trigonometry: MYP 4 teaches all three ratios ($\sin, \cos, \tan$); Beninese 4ème teaches strictly the cosine ($\cos$), reserving sine and tangent for 3ème.
  - Vectors: Beninese 4ème establishes a rigorous synthetic vector framework (direction, sense, norm, Chasles' relation $\vec{AB}+\vec{BC}=\vec{AC}$), providing an exceptional geometric foundation that enriches the IB algebraic vector perspective.
- **Harmonisation Strategy**: Introduce $\sin$ and $\tan$ as natural extensions of $\cos$ during the trigonometry unit; leverage the Beninese geometric vector foundation to provide intuitive depth for MYP column vectors.

#### Detailed Comparative Analysis (Troisième / MYP 5)

- **Harmonisation Strategy**: Run a harmonised core curriculum through Terms 1 and 2; in Term 3, provide targeted bifurcation:
  - Track A: BEPC examination workshops emphasizing 3-problem contextual scenarios, criterion rubrics ($C_a, C_m, C_o$), and non-calculator paper-and-pencil rigor;

#### Detailed Comparative Analysis (Seconde / DP 1)
- **Mathematical Convergence**: The core pillars of Seconde C/D—quadratic equations, functional analysis, 2D vector dot products, Al-Kashi theorem, and unit-circle radian trigonometry—match DP Topic 2 (Functions), Topic 3 (Geometry & Trig), and Topic 1 (Algebra).

- **Harmonisation Strategy**: Co-teach quadratics, functions, trigonometry, and dot products as an integrated master course. Schedule dedicated DP 1 modules for sequences and logarithmic foundations, while preserving the Beninese rigorous proofs in 3D geometry and metric properties.
