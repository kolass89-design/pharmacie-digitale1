# MYP 5 Mathematics — Mid-Term Test (Oak International School)

Print-ready assessment on **Linear Sequences** and **Arithmetic Sequences**, aligned to
IB MYP Criterion A (Knowing and Understanding) and Criterion D (Applying Mathematics in Real-Life Contexts).

## Deliverables

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `MYP5_Mathematics_MidTerm_Test.pdf` | Print-ready student paper — **exactly one A4 portrait page** |
| `MYP5_Mathematics_MidTerm_Test.docx` | Editable student paper (same layout) |
| `MYP5_Mathematics_MidTerm_Mark_Scheme.docx` | Teacher's mark scheme: answers, worked solutions, mark allocation, criterion mapping, misconceptions |

## Assessment structure (20 marks, 30 minutes, no calculator)

| Task | Focus | Criterion | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognising sequences; common difference; linear vs non-linear | A | 4 |
| 2 | nth-term formula, reverse engineering, term membership proof | A | 6 |
| 3 | Theatre seating (Cotonou) — modelling, sum, interpretation | D | 6 |
| 4 | Reasoning challenge: claim investigation, two sequences | A | 4 |

All answers were verified computationally (three independent passes); the single-page
layout was rendered and inspected (`gen_exam.py` regenerates the paper deterministically).
