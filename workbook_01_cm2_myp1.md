## SECTION B — BILINGUAL MATHEMATICAL LANGUAGE / GLOSSAIRE BILINGUE

### Technical Lexicon: Numbers, Fractions, Operations & Mensuration

| English Term | French Equivalent (*Terme Français*) | Mathematical Definition & Symbolic Example | Common False Friends & Translation Pitfalls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whole number** | *Nombre entier naturel* | A positive integer without decimals: $0, 1, 2, 3, \dots \in \mathbb{N}$. | Do not confuse with "integer" which includes negative numbers ($\mathbb{Z}$). |
| **Decimal mark / point** | *Virgule décimale* | Symbol separating whole units from tenths: e.g., $12.5$ (UK/IB) vs $12,5$ (Benin). | In English use a dot ($3.14$); in French use a comma ($3,14$). |
| **Numerator / Denominator** | *Numérateur / Dénominateur* | In fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, $a$ is the numerator (parts taken), $b$ is denominator (total parts). | Pronunciation: *numérateur* is on top; *dénominateur* names the fractional unit. |
| **Equivalent fractions** | *Fractions égales / équivalentes* | Fractions with equal value: $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{20}{30}$. | Always state the multiplying factor applied to both terms. |
| **Lowest terms / Irreducible** | *Fraction irréductible* | A fraction where numerator and denominator share no common divisor $> 1$: e.g., $\frac{3}{5}$. | In French, say *« rendre irréductible »*, not "simplifier au plus bas". |
| **Cost price / Selling price** | *Prix de revient / Prix de vente* | *Prix de revient* = Purchase price + transport/overhead fees; *Bénéfice* = Selling price $-$ Cost price. | *Frais* translates to "overheads" or "expenses", not "costs". |
| **Area / Perimeter** | *Aire (ou Surface) / Périmètre* | Perimeter is total boundary length ($P$ in m); Area is total surface enclosed ($A$ in $\text{m}^2$). | In English, "surface area" usually refers to 3D solids; for 2D, use "area". |
| **Capacity / Volume** | *Capacité / Volume* | Volume is the spatial extent ($\text{m}^3, \text{dm}^3$); Capacity is the fluid volume held ($\text{L}, \text{cL}$). | Crucial equivalence: $1\text{ dm}^3 = 1\text{ L} = 1\text{ kg}$ of pure water. |
| **Hectare (ha)** | *Hectare (ha)* | Agrarian unit of area equal to a square of $100\text{ m} \times 100\text{ m} = 10\ 000\text{ m}^2 = 1\text{ hm}^2$. | Indispensable for Beninese agricultural CEP problems. |

## SECTION C — SHARED CONCEPTUAL FOUNDATIONS

### Unit Theme: Fractions, Proportional Sharing and Commercial Mensuration

#### 1. Conceptual Introduction: The Multiplicative Nature of Fractions
A fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ represents two complementary mathematical operations:
1. **Partitioning**: Dividing a whole unit into $b$ identical equal sub-parts and selecting $a$ of those parts.
2. **Division Operator**: The exact quotient of $a$ divided by $b$.

$$\frac{a}{b} = a \div b \quad (b \neq 0)$$

In both the IB MYP and the Beninese curriculum, a fraction is an operator that can scale a quantity:

$$\text{Fraction of a Quantity} = \frac{a}{b} \times Q = \frac{a \times Q}{b} = a \times \frac{Q}{b}$$

#### 2. Visual Representation: The Number Line and Area Bar Model
Consider the fraction $\frac{3}{4}$:
- On a number line from $0$ to $1$, subdivide the unit interval into 4 segments of length $0.25$. The third tick mark represents $\frac{3}{4} = 0.75$.
- In a rectangular area of $100\text{ m}^2$, $\frac{3}{4}$ represents three $25\text{ m}^2$ quadrants = $75\text{ m}^2$.

#### 3. Worked Example: Method Comparison Across Programmes
**Problem**: An agricultural cooperative in Parakou harvests $1\ 200\text{ kg}$ of organic cashews. They sell $\frac{3}{5}$ of the harvest at $800\text{ CFA F}$ per kilogram. What is the total revenue?

- **The IB Method (Step-by-step Unitary Scaling)**:
  - Find $\frac{1}{5}$ of the harvest: $1\ 200 \div 5 = 240\text{ kg}$.
  - Multiply by the numerator: $240 \times 3 = 720\text{ kg}$ sold.
  - Calculate revenue: $720\text{ kg} \times 800\text{ CFA F/kg} = 576\ 000\text{ CFA F}$.
- **The Beninese APC Method (Standard Formulated Solution)**:
  - *Masse de cajou vendue* : $\frac{1\ 200 \times 3}{5} = \frac{3\ 600}{5} = 720\text{ kg}$.
  - *Recette totale* : $720 \times 800 = 576\ 000\text{ CFA F}$.
- **Synthesis**: Both methods yield the identical outcome. The IB approach emphasizes unit rate thinking, while the Beninese APC focuses on the formal fractional operator and compact calculation.

## SECTION D — CONTEXTUALISED BRIDGING ACTIVITIES

### Activity 1: The Dantokpa Market Grain Merchant

#### 1. Problem Situation
Madame Akpéné is a prominent grain merchant at the Dantokpa Market in Cotonou. She receives a delivery of $2.4\text{ tonnes}$ ($2\ 400\text{ kg}$) of high-grade maize from a farmer in Glazoué.
- Packaging: She packages $\frac{5}{8}$ of the maize into large $50\text{ kg}$ sacks for wholesale restaurants.
- Retail: She packages the remaining maize into small $2.5\text{ kg}$ retail bags for local family sales.
- Wholesale sacks sell for $18\ 000\text{ CFA F}$ each.
- Retail bags sell for $1\ 100\text{ CFA F}$ each.
- Transport overheads cost $45\ 000\text{ CFA F}$ for the entire shipment.

#### 2. The IB Perspective (Inquiry & Representation)
- Identify the fraction of maize remaining for retail sales:
  $$1 - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8}$$
- Construct a visual bar model partitioned into 8 equal units of $300\text{ kg}$ each.
- Wholesale portion (5 units): $5 \times 300\text{ kg} = 1\ 500\text{ kg}$.
- Retail portion (3 units): $3 \times 300\text{ kg} = 900\text{ kg}$.

#### 3. The Beninese APC Perspective (Structured Solution / *Démarche Raisonnée*)
- *Quantité de maïs pour la vente en gros* :
  $$\frac{2\ 400 \times 5}{8} = 1\ 500\text{ kg}$$
- *Nombre de sacs de $50\text{ kg}$* :
  $$1\ 500 \div 50 = 30\text{ sacs}$$
- *Recette de la vente en gros* :
  $$30 \times 18\ 000 = 540\ 000\text{ CFA F}$$
- *Quantité de maïs pour la vente au détail* :
  $$2\ 400 - 1\ 500 = 900\text{ kg}$$
- *Nombre de sachets de $2,5\text{ kg}$* :
  $$900 \div 2,5 = 360\text{ sachets}$$
- *Recette de la vente au détail* :
  $$360 \times 1\ 100 = 396\ 000\text{ CFA F}$$
- *Recette totale brute* :
  $$540\ 000 + 396\ 000 = 936\ 000\text{ CFA F}$$
- *Bénéfice net après déduction des frais de transport* :
  $$\text{Bénéfice} = 936\ 000 - 45\ 000 = 891\ 000\text{ CFA F}$$

#### 4. Comparison of Methods
- IB uses fraction subtraction to verify partition consistency ($\frac{5}{8} + \frac{3}{8} = 1$).
- Benin APC uses direct sequential arithmetic reasoning with explicit labels (*phrase explicative*) for each intermediate result.
